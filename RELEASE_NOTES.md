Sable 1.0.0 — the first Windows release.

- One library for books and light novels, from EPUB, PDF, MOBI and AZW3 files (also inside ZIPs). Your files are never changed.
- Series and volumes found from folders and file names, with editions such as Web Novel and Fan Translation. An EPUB and a PDF of the same volume show as one book.
- Cover grid, list, Continue reading, filters with opposites, reversible sorts, tags, favourites, half-star ratings, and a toggle to hide your downloaded collections.
- A Lithium-style reader with two pages side by side on wide windows, a few reading settings (font, size, spacing, margins, justify), contents, bookmarks, search in the book, full-screen pictures, and Next volume at the end.
- PDFs in one or two pages, with an optional dark-pages mode.
- Reading history and stats: time, pages, speed, streaks, books per month and more.
- OLED black and Starlight themes with gradient accents.

Download `Sable-v1.0.0-windows.zip`, unzip it to `E:\Pictures\`, and run `Sable\App\Sable.exe`. (`selftest-screenshots.zip` is only the build's own check and can be ignored.)
