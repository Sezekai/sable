Sable 1.3.1

- Closing Sable is now instant: the window goes straight away and the app saves and ends behind it (before, it waited for the book engine, folder watching and background work to wind down one by one).
- Ctrl+W closes Sable, on any screen including the reader.
- New logo without the two lines under the moon. It's now the app icon on Windows and Android, and the logo at the top of the sidebar (and the phone's library).
- The Android app is now signed with Sable's own permanent key, so from this version on each update installs over the last one and keeps the phone's library, downloads and pairing.

If you installed the 1.3.0 APK, uninstall it once before installing this one (it was signed with a temporary key). Nothing is lost that the next sync won't bring back, apart from downloads and pairing.

PC: replace the `App` folder in `E:\Pictures\Sable\` with the new one. Phone: install `Sable-v1.3.1-android.apk`.
