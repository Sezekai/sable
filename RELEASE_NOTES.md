Sable 1.5.0

Reader
- Scroll mode is now the whole book as one long page. Chapters follow each other with a divider naming the next chapter, and scrolling uses the browser's own smooth scrolling, so it's quick, never sticks, and needs no extra push between chapters. Parts of the book load as you get near them and are let go far behind, so long books stay light. Switching between pages and scrolling keeps your place.
- Pictures: click one, then use the arrow keys to go through every picture in the book. The page behind doesn't move, and at the last picture it loops back to the first. Small decorations are skipped.
- The controls no longer appear when you move the mouse. A click shows or hides them. Opening a picture doesn't bring them up either; clicking the picture does. Every keyboard shortcut still works, including while a picture is open.

Library
- Mouse-wheel scrolling was rebuilt to feel like a web browser: each notch glides about 120 pixels, and spinning the wheel quickly piles the notches up so it covers ground fast without stuttering. This is in the library, book and series pages, history, stats, settings and the reader's lists.
- A click anywhere on the bottom bar opens the log.
- Sable now really starts maximised. Windows' own first draw of the window was un-maximising it.

Descriptions and covers
- "Find online" on a book or series page opens a search: change the title or author, choose Apple Books, Google Books, Open Library or everywhere, and pick the description you want from what's found.
- The Cover button (next to Edit details) has: Find online (choose from covers found on Apple Books, Google Books and Open Library, and search again under another name), Use a picture (paste, drag in or choose a file), Frame it (drag the picture to choose which part shows, such as the top of a tall cover) and Use the book's own. The button on the cover itself is gone.
- A series' own cover now also shows on its tile in the library.
- Renaming a series keeps you on its page under the new name, and its description and cover come with it.

PC: replace the `App` folder in `E:\Pictures\Sable\` with the new one. Phone: install `Sable-v1.5.0-android.apk` over the old one (both need 1.5 for syncing).
