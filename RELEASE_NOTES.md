Sable 1.1.0

Reader
- Scrolling is smooth: each notch of the mouse wheel glides a set distance (choose it under Aa → Scroll distance) instead of jumping. In page mode, one notch turns one page.
- No scrollbar under the bars any more; the position shows in the status line. Scroll mode now reads "page 1 of 14" instead of "page 0".
- Bars, panels and the status line are bigger, and Reader controls size (Aa, or Settings → Look) sets them anywhere from 80% to 200%.
- Big pictures fill the page: the whole window in one-page view, half of it in two-page view.
- Click a picture, then use ← → (or the mouse's side buttons) to go through every picture in the book; the page follows along. Esc or a click outside closes it.
- The mouse's back and forward buttons turn pages (or, if you prefer, Back leaves the book).
- Every reading setting has a ↺ button to put it back to its default, plus Reset all.

Library and window
- Search boxes fill the toolbar and line up with the buttons next to them.
- The sidebar highlights the page you're on.
- The mouse's back and forward buttons move through where you've been (library, books, series, History, Stats, Settings).
- App size in Settings → Look scales the whole window (80% to 160%).

Themes
- Both themes can be edited: click any colour to change it, pick a preset or your own accent gradient. Changes are saved into the theme, and "Reset … to default" puts it back.
- Every setting shows a ↺ button when it's been changed, to put it back to its default.

Fixes
- "Making covers" failed with an "unsendable" error. Fixed.
- Locked ZIPs (86, Record of Wortenia War, Kobold King and others) open now: Sable uses the password in "Password for many rars.txt", or ones you add in Settings → Library.
- A damaged MOBI (Fahrenheit 451) is still added, named from its file.
- Volumes written like "V10" or "-V2" (DanMachi) are numbered properly.
- The library scan reads books on several cores at once, so a full scan is much faster.
- If Sable ever stops unexpectedly again, it leaves a crash report in its logs folder and mentions it at the next start. The crash you had left no report, so this is there to catch it next time.

Download `Sable-v1.1.0-windows.zip`, then replace the `App` folder in `E:\Pictures\Sable\` with the new one. Your library and settings are kept.
